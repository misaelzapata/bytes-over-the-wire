NOTE ON BANKSY: Banksy photos on Commons are cited in the catalog as visual
reference only and were deliberately NOT downloaded — the UK grants no freedom
of panorama for 2D graphic works, so the underlying artwork stays under the
artist's copyright even when a photo is CC-licensed. Use only for study, not
redistribution of the artwork image.
